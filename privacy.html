Privacy

Provenance Counseling LLC runs this site. This page says what we collect here, why, and how to have it removed.

What we collect

  • When you fill out the contact form: your name, email, phone number, which therapist you're interested in, and your message.
  • When you visit: a count of page visits, with no cookies and nothing that names you (Plausible).

That's all. There are no ads on this site, and we never sell your information.

Why

To get back to you about therapy, and to see which pages people read.

Who holds it

  • Netlify hosts the site and passes your form message to us.
  • Plausible counts visits.

To be removed

Call or text us at (608) 572-7592, or send a note through the contact form, and we'll delete your message.
